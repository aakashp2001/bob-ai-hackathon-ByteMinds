# public-appeal

For the supplied case number:

1. Call `get_person_profile` through MCP.
2. Use only profile fields marked or understood as family-provided.
3. Generate a concise professional appeal containing the case number, name, age, physical description, clothing, last known date and location, and fictional contact details.
4. Do not include speculative investigator tips or CCTV descriptions as facts.
5. Do not state or imply that any sighting confirms identity.
